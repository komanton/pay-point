Use Pay on Way to organize your phone numbers, account numbers, and IBANs based on your current geolocation.

When you add a new payment method, our app automatically detects your location. The next time you open the app, it will show only the phone numbers within a 30-meter radius of your current location. This is useful, for example, when you want to pay for coffee at a specific location that accepts payments only via phone numbers.
